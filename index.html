Projects

Personal Projects

I build and maintain personal, open-source projects spanning predictive modelling, natural language processing, and data engineering.

🤖 Machine Learning & Prediction: I developed predictive models applied to finance and sports: Financial-ML, an equity selection system using market data, fundamentals, and sentiment indicators, achieving 20.2% annual returns and a 0.93 Sharpe ratio; and footAI, an end-to-end system predicting match outcomes across Europe's top five leagues by combining Elo ratings with Random Forest, reaching 50.5% accuracy and 37.4% draw recall.

🔤 Natural Language Processing: I fine-tuned FinBERT for financial sentiment analysis, building a multi-task architecture that learns jointly across news, social media, and forum text to reach 85.4% accuracy, adapting a language model to a specific, noisy domain rather than just prompting a general one.

📈 Data Engineering & Visualization: I created Meteomat, a probabilistic weather dashboard blending ECMWF ensemble (EPS) and HRES forecasts for sharper estimates with uncertainty bands, covering worldwide locations with a marine tab (swell, waves, sea-surface temperature), deployed at meteo.matorras.com. I also developed PulseTransit, a real-time pipeline turning GPS and ETA data from Santander's public buses into a live map of the city's transit network, deployed at tustracker.matorras.com, and a macroeconomics dashboard visualizing IMF economic indicators across countries with interactive geospatial visualization.

Professional Projects

At Centro Tecnológico CTC, I work on projects spanning predictive modelling, infrastructure analytics, and internal LLM tooling.

Predictive Modelling & Time-Series: I'm building predictive models for battery State of Health and Remaining Useful Life for GIRA's electric vehicle battery packs (Batechain), engineering per-cycle waveform encoders paired with cross-cycle sequence models (CNN, LSTM, and Transformer), and benchmarking them against pretrained time-series foundation models (PatchTST, Chronos) adapted as interchangeable encoders.

Infrastructure Analytics: I'm developing predictive analytics for datacenter infrastructure (Protwin), combining time-series forecasting with anomaly detection to flag potential hardware failures from historical monitoring data before they occur.

Internal LLM Tooling: I've also built an internal system that runs local, self-hosted LLMs over private company data, powering a retrieval-augmented knowledge base and a conversational agent that decides between semantic search, direct lookup, and filtered queries depending on what's being asked.
